Frequently Asked Questions

Our FAQ section is created to guide you through everything you need to know about our bakery.

Do you bake products fresh every day?

Yes, we bake our products fresh every day to ensure the best taste, quality, and freshness for all customers.

What ingredients do you use in your baking?

We use high-quality ingredients like fresh flour, eggs, butter, sugar, and natural flavors. Everything is made with care for the best taste.

How can I place a custom order?

You can place a custom order by contacting us, sharing your requirements, and confirming details like design, flavor, and date.

How far in advance should I order?

We recommend placing your order at least 2–5 days in advance to ensure availability and proper preparation time.

Do you offer bulk or event orders?

Yes, we offer bulk and event orders for birthdays, weddings, corporate events, and special occasions with customized options available.
